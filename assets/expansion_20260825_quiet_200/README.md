# Quiet Glyph expansion reserve

Isolated expansion-only package containing 137 new Joker concepts and 67 new
Blind concepts. Combined with the approved base package, the catalog reaches
200 Jokers and 100 Blinds.

## Contents

- `catalog/catalog.json`: full names, reserve mechanics, families, visual
  metaphors, unique visual signatures, and generation subjects.
- `jokers/`: 137 expansion Joker PNG masters.
- `blinds_concept/`: 67 expansion Blind PNG masters.
- `manifest.json`: paths, hashes, review state, safe-band state, and combined
  counts.
- `contact_sheets/`: labeled and hidden-label full/band/150px review surfaces.
- `reports/`: safe-band, perceptual-similarity, and regeneration records.

## Rendering contract

- 1024×1024 PNG on `#030308`.
- All strong subject pixels inside `y=312..711`.
- Quiet flat glyphs: one grouped subject plus at most one modifier.
- No glass, particle fields, scenes, characters, prose, or incorrect score
  labels.
- Expansion Blinds use muted dark-red outlines only.

## Differentiation gates

- Catalog IDs, Chinese/English names, reserve mechanics, and visual-signature
  keys are unique across the base and expansion catalogs.
- 150px labeled and hidden-label contact sheets received a final visual PASS.
- Cropped-mask perceptual hashing found no pair with pHash distance ≤8.
- Multiple targeted regeneration rounds replaced confusing, character-like,
  scene-like, text-bearing, or low-legibility outputs.

This package is not connected to runtime mechanics or balance data. It is
reserved for future expansion-card development and leaves all previous packages
and runtime assets untouched.
