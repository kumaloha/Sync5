are not the final asset manifest. Shared generated-output discovery made the
batch 1–5 mappings unreliable, and batch 3–4 temporarily used non-model local
fallbacks. Every affected destination was subsequently overwritten from an
explicit built-in image-generation result in the primary task, then resized and
reviewed. `../manifest.json` and the final contact sheets are the authoritative
delivery record.

per-asset prompt/concern provenance for its 14 Blind concepts.
